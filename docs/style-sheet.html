Invictus Team

Style sheet · v0.1 · derived from the VI monogram. Ink and paper, with one signature gold for highlights.

Logo

Black on light surfaces, white on dark. Never place the black mark on a dark background or recolour it. Keep clear space of at least the height of the “invictus” band on every side.

App icon

iOS ships three variants that follow the Home Screen appearance. Android uses an adaptive icon with the mark inside the safe zone. At small sizes the script is hard to read, so a vector source would sharpen it.

Colour

Primary actions use ink, which inverts per theme. Gold is the signature highlight: current tab, next up, play, links and progress. Gold is a placeholder until the colour from the current Invictus app is confirmed. Light mode uses a deeper gold for text so it stays readable on white.

Rules

  • One primary (ink) button per screen.
  • Glass belongs to navigation and floating controls; content rows stay calm.
  • Gold highlights, it never carries body text or large fills. One gold focal point per card.
  • Respect Reduce Transparency, Reduce Motion and Dynamic Type.
  • Dark mode is true black so the white monogram and OLED screens look their best.
