Privacy Policy

Invictus Team app · Effective November 25, 2026

The Invictus Team app is a private app for members of the Invictus team. It holds team training, resources and events, and a Leaderboard where members log the memberships and orders they make. This page explains what the app stores about you and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email address, and your password, which is stored only in hashed form so no one can read it.
  • Your profile photo, if you add one.
  • Sales you log on the Leaderboard: whether each was a membership or an order, and when.
  • Notification settings, and if you turn on alerts, a push token that lets us send them to your device.
  • Your appearance settings (light or dark), kept on your device.
  • Team profiles: team admins can publish a profile for a member, with names, photos, city and a short bio. Photos are only uploaded from the device when someone picks them.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone outside the team.
  • We don't use your location, contacts or microphone.

Who can see it

Only signed-in team members. They see your name, your photo and your sales totals on the Leaderboard and in sale alerts. Your email address is visible only to team admins, who can also remove a sale logged by mistake.

A team profile can be shared with someone outside the team through a private link that stops working after 7 days. The link shows only that profile.

Where it's kept

Your information is stored with Supabase, our database provider, in the United States. Alerts are delivered through Expo's push service and Apple or Google's notification services. The web version of the app is hosted by Vercel. These providers process data only to run the app.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: open Account and tap Delete account. Your account, name, photo, sales and settings are removed right away and can't be recovered.

Children

The app is for adult team members and isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
