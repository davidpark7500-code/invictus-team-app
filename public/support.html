Invictus Team support

The Invictus Team app is for members of the Invictus team. If something isn't working, email hello@holisticcreativestudios.com and we'll get back to you.

Signing in

Already on Pulse Board? Sign in with the same email and password you use there. New to the team? Tap Create an account and enter the team code from your upline.

Logged a sale by mistake?

Tap Undo next to it in Live activity within 15 minutes. After that, use Fix this week's count on the Leaderboard.

Deleting your account

Open Account and tap Delete account. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
